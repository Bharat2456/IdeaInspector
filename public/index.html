What you get

A blunt first pass, then a real conversation.

IdeaInspector is built to reject weak ideas fast and sharpen promising ones, not to flatter you.

01

Investor-style memo

Score, category breakdown, market and competitors, business model verdict, risk register, roadmap, comparables and investor readiness.

02

A score you can audit

Every report says what evidence it used and how confident it is, and names the biggest risk and what would move the score.

03

AI consultant chat

Ask why you scored low, run a SWOT or pricing review, and get consulting-grade advice grounded in your actual report.

04

Re-run in place

When the consultant finally understands your idea, re-run the report from the chat. The same report updates; no clutter of old copies.

05

Download as PDF

A full memo PDF with clickable sources, or a one-page summary. Your reports stay available in the Dashboard.

06

Any AI, your key

Use Claude, GPT, Gemini, Llama, DeepSeek or a local model. Paste your own key and pick any model. No accounts.

How it works

From a rough idea to a sharper one.

1

Describe the idea

Write it in plain words. Add links, a pitch PDF or images if you have them.

2

Get the report

A scored diligence memo appears in seconds, with the reasoning behind every number.

3

Talk to the consultant

Open as many chats as you like on that report. Challenge the score, fix misreadings, ask for frameworks.

4

Re-run when it clicks

Review the updated description the consultant understood, then refresh the same report with one click.

The consultant

Powered by a full management-consulting toolkit.

The chat is wired to the open-source business-consulting skill pack. For each question it loads the frameworks that fit, such as market sizing, competitive analysis, pricing or unit economics, and applies them to your idea.
